# Editing your website, a plain-English guide

Welcome, Keith. This is the guide to updating your own website. You don't need to know any
code, everything is done through a simple visual editor. If you can fill in a form, you can
edit your site.

> **The one thing to remember:** you can't break anything permanently. Every change is saved
> with a full history, so if something ever looks wrong, Ron can put it back in seconds. Edit
> with confidence.

---

## 2. How saving works (important)

When you make a change:

1. You edit the text or image in the editor.
2. You click **Save** (top right).
3. Behind the scenes, the website rebuilds itself. **Your change appears live in about a
   minute**, give it 60 seconds, then refresh the public page to see it.

So: *edit → Save → wait a minute → it's live.* That's the whole cycle. Nothing goes live until
you click Save, and there's no separate "publish to the internet" step to forget.

## 4. Common things you'll want to do

### ✏️ Change some text on a page
1. Open **Page copy** and click the page (e.g. **Homepage** or **About page**).
2. Find the box with the text you want to change and type over it.
3. Click **Save**. Done.

### 📞 Change your phone number, email, hours or address
1. Open **Site settings → Contact, hours, brand**.
2. Edit the field (e.g. **Opening hours**, **Phone**, **Email**).
3. Click **Save**. This updates *everywhere* it appears on the site at once, you only change
   it in this one place.

### 🖼️ Swap a photo
1. Open the page or item with the photo (e.g. **Page copy → Homepage → Images**, or a
   **Service**).
2. Click the image field, then **Choose an image**. You can pick one already in your library
   or **upload a new one** by dragging it in.
3. **Always fill in the "alt text" box next to it**, a short, plain description of what's in
   the photo (e.g. *"Cockapoo after a full groom"*). This helps blind visitors and Google.
4. Click **Save**.

> *You don't need to resize photos first, the website automatically creates fast, optimised
> versions of anything you upload. A freshly uploaded photo may take one extra rebuild
> (another minute) to look its sharpest. Big, bright, in-focus photos work best.*

### ⭐ Add a customer review
1. Open **Reviews** and click **New review** (the "+" / "Add" button).
2. Fill in: the customer's name (e.g. *Sarah M.*), where they live, the dog's name and breed,
   the service, the star rating, the date, and what they said.
3. Tick **"Show on every page?"** for your best 3, those appear in the strip under every page.
4. Click **Save**.

> ⚠️ **Only add real reviews from real customers, with their permission.** Made-up reviews
> aren't allowed by Google and can get the site penalised.

### 🐶 Add a groom story (before & after)
1. Open **Groom Stories** and click **New**.
2. Fill in the dog's name, breed, the service, the area they live, and the date.
3. Write the **Full story** in the big box, a few short paragraphs in your own words.
4. Add a **Before photo** and an **After photo** (these should be two *different* pictures).
5. Click **Save**.

### 💷 Change a price
1. Open **Services** and click the service.
2. Edit the **From £** figure and the **Price tiers**.
3. Click **Save**. (Prices show on the service page and the pricing page automatically.)

### 📍 Edit a local area page
1. Open **Areas covered** and click the area (e.g. **Horbury**).
2. Edit the **Intro line**, the **Body paragraphs**, or the **Area FAQs**.
3. Click **Save**.

## 5. A few tips for good results

- **Keep it in British English** (colour, centre, neighbour), it matches the rest of the site.
- **Short paragraphs read best** on a phone, where most people will see your site.
- When a field says *"HTML allowed"* or shows things in `<angle brackets>`, leave the brackets
  alone and just edit the words around them, they control colours and links.
- **Don't paste from Word** directly into long-text boxes (it can bring hidden formatting).
  Paste into Notes first, then copy from there, or just type it in.
- Every photo should have a short **alt text** describing what's in it.

## 6. If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The editor page won't load | Refresh. If it still won't, you may need to sign in again. |
| It won't let you sign in | Contact Ron, it's usually a quick fix on his side. |
| Your change hasn't appeared yet | Wait a full minute and refresh the public page (not the editor). Rebuilds take ~60 seconds. |
| You changed something and it looks wrong | Don't panic. Change it back and Save, or message Ron, who can restore any previous version instantly. |
| You're not sure about a field | Most fields have a small grey hint under them explaining what they do. When in doubt, leave it. |
